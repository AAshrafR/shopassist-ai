# ShopAssist Return and Refund Policy

## 1. Return Window
Customers have thirty (30) calendar days from the date of package delivery to request a return or replacement for eligible products. Items returned after 30 days are ineligible for refunds or store credits unless covered by an extended manufacturer warranty.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for a full refund:
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the exact condition received.
- Products must be in their original manufacturer packaging with all accessories, user manuals, tags, and protective seals intact.
- Proof of purchase (such as Order Confirmation ID or receipt) must accompany all return requests.

### Non-Returnable Items
- Personalized or custom-engraved merchandise.
- Perishable goods, grocery items, and gift cards.
- Intimate apparel, swimwear, and opened health/personal care items for hygiene reasons.
- Downloadable software licenses and digital media codes.

## 3. Return Process
1. **Initiate Return**: Visit your ShopAssist account dashboard or notify customer support via chat or email at `returns@shopassist.ai` with your Order ID and reason for return.
2. **Prepaid Shipping Label**: For damaged, defective, or incorrect items, ShopAssist generates a complimentary prepaid shipping return label. For voluntary returns (buyer remorse or incorrect sizing), a flat $5.99 return shipping deduction applies to the refund.
3. **Inspection**: Once delivered to our warehouse, our quality assurance team conducts an inspection within 2 business days.

## 4. Refund Methods and Processing Times
- **Original Payment Method**: Refunds are issued back to the original credit card, debit card, or PayPal account within 3 to 5 business days post-inspection.
- **Store Credit**: Customers electing store credit receive an instant ShopAssist Digital Gift Card with an extra 5% bonus balance.
- **Bank Processing**: Depending on your financial institution, funds may take an additional 2 to 7 business days to appear on your bank statement.

## 5. Damaged or Defective Items
If your merchandise arrives damaged or defective, please submit high-resolution photos and report the problem within 48 hours of delivery. A replacement will be expedited at zero cost, or an immediate full refund will be processed.
