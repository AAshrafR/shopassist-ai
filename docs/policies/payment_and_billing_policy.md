# ShopAssist Payment, Invoicing and Billing Policy

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
ShopAssist accepts the following secure payment options:
- **Major Credit and Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB, and UnionPay.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal, and Venmo.
- **Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)**: Klarna and Afterpay (available on orders between $35.00 and $1,500.00, split into 4 interest-free installments).
- **ShopAssist Gift Cards**: Digital gift cards and store promotional credits.

We do not accept personal checks, money orders, cash-on-delivery (COD), or cryptocurrencies.

## 2. Invoicing and Receipts
- An itemized PDF invoice is automatically generated upon order completion and transmitted to your registered email address.
- Invoices detail subtotal amounts, applicable sales tax by jurisdiction, promotional discounts, shipping fees, and payment transaction IDs.
- Registered customers can download official invoices at any time under the **Orders & Billing** section of their user profile.

## 3. Payment Authorizations and Charges
- When you submit an order, your bank places a temporary authorization hold on your account for the total order amount.
- The actual capture of funds occurs only when your order has been packed and prepared for carrier pickup.
- In case of cancelled items, the pre-authorization hold drops off automatically according to your issuing bank's policies (usually within 24 to 72 hours).

## 4. Failed Payments and Disputed Charges
- Common causes of payment declines include incorrect CVV/expiration date, billing address mismatch (AVS check failed), or international transaction restrictions.
- In the event of a disputed transaction or chargeback, please communicate directly with our support team first to resolve the issue swiftly without incurring administrative chargeback arbitration fees.
