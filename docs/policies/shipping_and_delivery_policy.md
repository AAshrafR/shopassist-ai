# ShopAssist Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods and Delivery Timelines
ShopAssist AI offers multiple shipping tiers across domestic and international destinations:

| Shipping Tier | Estimated Transit Time | Cost | Free Shipping Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Ground | 3 - 5 Business Days | $4.99 | Free on orders over $50.00 |
| Expedited Two-Day | 2 Business Days | $9.99 | Free on orders over $150.00 |
| Priority Overnight | 1 Business Day (Next Day) | $19.99 | Not eligible for threshold |
| International Standard | 7 - 14 Business Days | $24.99 | Varies by country duties |

## 2. Order Processing & Fulfillment
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST Monday through Friday are processed and dispatched on the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST or during weekends and observed federal holidays will be dispatched on the subsequent business day.
- Once dispatched, an automated shipping confirmation email with carrier tracking numbers (FedEx, UPS, USPS, DHL) is generated.

## 3. Shipping Address Modifications
- Address modifications can be processed within thirty (30) minutes of order placement while the order status remains `Pending` or `Processing`.
- Once an order transitions to `Shipped` or `Out for Delivery`, carrier rerouting fees of $12.00 may apply, subject to carrier discretion.

## 4. Lost, Stolen, or Delayed Packages
- **Delayed Shipments**: In the event of severe weather or carrier delays exceeding 3 business days past the projected delivery window, customers qualify for a shipping fee reimbursement.
- **Lost in Transit**: If tracking shows no activity for five (5) consecutive business days, contact our support team. We will immediately initiate a carrier trace and dispatch a complimentary replacement.
- **Stolen Packages / Porch Piracy**: If an order is marked `Delivered` but cannot be located, please verify around entryways and with neighbors. If unlocated after 24 hours, contact customer support to submit a signature-verification dispute ticket.
