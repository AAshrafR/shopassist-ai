# ShopAssist Order Cancellation Policy

## 1. Cancellation Grace Period
Customers can self-cancel an order free of charge within **60 minutes** of order placement, provided the order is still in `Pending` or `Submitted` status.

## 2. Cancellation Process
1. Log into your ShopAssist account.
2. Navigate to **Order History** and locate the order you wish to cancel.
3. If eligible, click the **Cancel Order** button.
4. Alternatively, request order cancellation immediately via ShopAssist AI chat assistant or customer support by quoting the Order ID (e.g., `ORD-1001`).

## 3. Post-Fulfillment Window
- Once an order transitions to `Processing`, `Packed`, or `Shipped`, automated fulfillment systems cannot halt shipment.
- In this situation, please wait for the order delivery and then initiate an easy return under our 30-day Return Policy.
- Refusing delivery at the doorstep may result in carrier return-to-sender delay and a restocking fee of 10%.

## 4. Cancellation Fees
- There are **no cancellation fees** for cancellations requested within the authorized window.
- Custom-made or personalized items cannot be cancelled once production has commenced (typically 2 hours after order submission).
