# ShopAssist Account Security and Profile Management Policy

## 1. Account Creation and Verification
- Customers can create a personal ShopAssist AI account with a valid email address and secure password.
- Passwords must contain a minimum of 8 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one numerical digit, and one special character.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via SMS or Authenticator App is recommended and can be activated under Account Settings.

## 2. Password Reset and Recovery
- If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password** on the login portal.
- A secure, single-use password recovery link will be dispatched to your registered email address and remains valid for fifteen (15) minutes.
- If you encounter issues receiving the email, check your spam/junk folder or contact support to verify account ownership.

## 3. Profile Information and Shipping Addresses
- Customers may update email addresses, phone numbers, and default shipping/billing addresses under the **Profile Settings** page.
- Changes made to saved addresses will apply to future orders only; modifications to existing active orders must be initiated directly with customer service or the chat assistant prior to dispatch.

## 4. Account Deletion and Privacy Rights
- In accordance with GDPR and CCPA regulations, customers can request full account deletion and personal data erasure.
- Deletion requests take up to 30 days to process and are irreversible.
- To submit a formal deletion or data privacy request, contact `privacy@shopassist.ai` or ask the support assistant to open a privacy ticket.
